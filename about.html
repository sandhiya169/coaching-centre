Our story

We started because a student got lost in a crowd

Our founder watched a bright Class 10 student fail Physics in an impersonal hall of 120. The teacher never knew he was struggling.

So we built the exact opposite: a classroom small enough that nobody can hide and nobody gets missed. Every batch is strictly capped at 15, every student has a dedicated mentor, and parents hear from us each month, not just when marks fall.
